## 🎙️ MASTROAI LIVE (AUDIO RECORDING)

### **Q: Why does the app ask for microphone permission?**
A: MastroAI Live needs access to your microphone to record your tactical instructions.

### **Q: What should I say when recording?**
A: Speak naturally, like you're coaching on the field. Be specific about what you want to improve.

**Example:**
```
"The team is losing the ball too quickly in midfield. 
We need better first-touch control and more passing options.
Let's focus on possession drills with tight passing triangles."
```

### **Q: How long should my recording be?**
A: 30-60 seconds is ideal. Too short = unclear instructions, too long = takes longer to analyze.

### **Q: What language should I use?**
A: French is recommended for best results. English and Spanish also work.

### **Q: The app says "Impossible d'accéder au microphone (#144)" - what does this mean?**
A: Your browser can't access your microphone. Solutions:
1. Check if you denied permission (check browser settings)
2. Restart your browser
3. Restart your computer
4. Try a different browser
5. Try on a different device

### **Q: The analysis takes too long. Is it broken?**
A: No, Gemini AI sometimes takes 5-10 seconds. If it takes >30 seconds:
1. Click "Réessayer" to retry
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try a different network
4. Try recording shorter instruction (30s instead of 60s)

### **Q: Can I edit the recommendations?**
A: Not yet, but it's coming in a future update.

## 📸 MASTROAI VISION (IMAGE ANALYSIS)

### **Q: What images work best?**
A: Clear, straight-on photos of the team's formation. Works best with:
- Well-lit images
- All 11 players visible
- Team wearing same color uniforms
- Players in tactical formation

### **Q: What image formats are supported?**
A: JPG, PNG, and WEBP formats. File size up to 10MB.

### **Q: Can I upload video instead of images?**
A: Not in beta version. Images only for now.

### **Q: The app says "Formation not detected" - why?**
A: The image might:
- Show fewer than 11 players
- Have poor lighting
- Be taken at odd angle
- Have clutter in background

Try again with a clearer image.

### **Q: Can I analyze a match recording?**
A: Not yet. For now, MastroAI Vision works with still images only. Video analysis is coming.

### **Q: What happens to my photos?**
A: Images are analyzed by Gemini AI but not stored. We respect your privacy.

## 📊 SESSION GENERATOR

### **Q: What themes are available?**
A: 9 themes:
- Possession
- Pressing  
- Transitions
- Centre
- Ailes (Flanks)
- Controle
- Vitesse (Speed)
- Phases
- 1v1

### **Q: What's the difference between load levels?**
A: Load determines exercise intensity:
- **Recovery:** Easy, low intensity
- **Moderate:** Medium intensity
- **High:** Intense, high effort

Pick based on team's current fitness level and schedule.

### **Q: What are the different tactical schools?**
A: 7 philosophical approaches to football:
- **Française:** Technical, possession-based
- **Espagnole:** Tiki-taka, quick passing
- **Allemande:** Structured, physical
- **Hollandaise:** Total football, flexible roles
- **Brésilienne:** Creative, individual skill
- **Argentine:** Aggressive pressing
- **Italienne:** Defensive, tactical awareness

Each school generates different training exercises.

### **Q: How long are generated sessions?**
A: Sessions are typically 45-60 minutes. You'll see the duration before finalizing.

### **Q: Can I customize the generated session?**
A: Yes! You can:
- Select/deselect exercises
- Add exercises from MastroAI Live
- Reorder exercises
- Save your favorites

### **Q: Where are my sessions saved?**
A: Sessions save to your browser's local storage. They persist between visits.

### **Q: Can I export sessions?**
A: PDF export coming in next update!

## 🔧 TECHNICAL & TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Q: What browser should I use?**
A: Any modern browser works:
- **Best:** Chrome/Edge 126+
- **Good:** Firefox 125+, Safari 17+
- **Works:** Safari 16+, older versions

Try Chrome if you have issues.

### **Q: The app is slow. How do I fix it?**
A: Try these:
1. Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
2. Clear browser cache
3. Close other browser tabs
4. Restart your browser
5. Try WiFi instead of mobile data
6. Try a different browser
